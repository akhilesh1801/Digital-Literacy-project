# Task-5 Case Study: Cybercrime Awareness

## Overview
This task was divided into two parts:
1. Writing a detailed case study (200–250 words) on a chosen cybercrime type.  
2. Reflecting on the importance of digital communication and awareness in preventing such crimes.  


## Part A – Case Study on Phishing
I chose **Phishing** as the cybercrime type and wrote a case study focusing on how a college student could be targeted.  

### Summary
Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers impersonate trusted entities to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information. In the case study, a student received a fraudulent email disguised as an official university notice. The attacker:
1. Designed a convincing email with the university’s logo.  
2. Sent it to multiple students, urging urgent action.  
3. Redirected victims to a fake login page.  
4. Collected usernames and passwords.  

The student entered their credentials, allowing the attacker to access personal data, academic records, and financial details. College students are often targeted due to limited awareness of online scams. The consequences included compromised accounts, financial loss, and emotional stress. This highlights the importance of digital literacy and cautious email handling.

## Part B – Reflection
Poor digital communication can amplify cybercrime risks. For example, if a student shares sensitive login details over unsecured messaging apps, attackers can easily exploit this information. Such negligence can lead to identity theft, financial fraud, or reputational damage. The situation could be avoided by practicing secure communication—verifying sources, using official channels, and never sharing confidential data casually. Responsible digital communication is therefore essential for protecting both personal and institutional security.
