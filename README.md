# Task-4 Email Etiquette & Social Media Responsibility

##  Overview
This task focused on practicing professional email writing and responsible social media use. It was divided into two parts:
1. Writing two professional emails for academic and career contexts.
2. Creating a checklist of Do’s and Don’ts for social media behavior as a college student.

##  Part A – Professional Emails
Two professional email drafts were prepared and saved in this folder:
- **Email 1**: Requesting an extension on an assignment submission deadline (student → professor).
- **Email 2**: Expressing interest in a summer internship opportunity (student → internship coordinator).

Both emails follow professional standards:
- Clear subject lines  
- Formal greetings  
- Well-structured body text  
- Proper sign-offs  


##  Part B – Social Media Do's and Don'ts
A checklist file was created with at least 5 Do’s and 5 Don’ts for responsible social media use.  


##  Project Report
Poor digital communication can lead to serious consequences. For example, a student once shared inappropriate comments about their professor on social media, which quickly spread among peers and faculty. This damaged the student’s reputation and resulted in disciplinary action. The situation could have been avoided by addressing concerns privately and respectfully through email instead of posting publicly. Practicing professional communication and following social media guidelines helps maintain credibility and fosters healthier academic relationships.
